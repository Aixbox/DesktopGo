## Design Context

### Users

Windows desktop users who want a fast local launcher for apps, files, and search. They use DesktopGo as a quick-access layer over the desktop, usually in short, repeatable bursts where speed and clarity matter more than decoration.

### Brand Personality

Calm, efficient, dependable. The product should feel like a refined desktop utility: confident enough to expose system-level actions, restrained enough to stay out of the way.

### Aesthetic Direction

Lean into a clean desktop-tool aesthetic with light and dark theme support, soft surfaces, subtle translucency, and compact information density. Avoid loud gradients, gimmicky glass panels, or web-dashboard styling that feels detached from a native desktop workflow.

### Design Principles

1. Prioritize operational clarity over visual novelty.
2. Surface real runtime and system information whenever the UI implies it.
3. Keep layouts compact but breathable, with clear grouping and strong scanability.
4. Make support, diagnostics, and project-entry actions one click away.
5. Use restrained accenting and motion so the interface feels polished, not theatrical.
